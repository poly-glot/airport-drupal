About Meridian

Careers

Around 3,100 people work at Meridian. Here is what it is like, and what we are hiring for now.

Demonstration note

These roles are fictional and are not open for applications.

Working at Meridian

Shifts that fit

The airport runs 24 hours, so many roles are shift-based. We publish rotas 6 weeks ahead and you can swap shifts with colleagues through the staff app.

Getting to work

Free staff parking at Mid-Stay, and half-price rail travel from Birmingham Central for everyone on our payroll.

Training

Every new starter gets paid training before their first shift. Most of our managers started in frontline roles.

Pay and benefits

  • Pay reviewed every April
  • 25 days' holiday plus bank holidays, rising to 28 with service
  • Pension with up to 8% employer contribution
  • Free meals on shift in the staff canteen

How to apply

  1. Read the full role description and check you meet the requirements.
  2. Apply online with your CV and a short covering statement. We do not use automated scoring.
  3. If you are shortlisted, we invite you to an interview at the airport within 2 weeks of the closing date.
  4. Before you start, we carry out the background checks needed for an airport security pass. This takes 4 to 6 weeks.

Adjustments

Tell us if you need any adjustments to apply or to attend an interview. We will make them. We guarantee an interview to disabled applicants who meet the essential requirements.
