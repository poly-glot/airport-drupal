Cookies

One strictly necessary cookie at most. No analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Please note

Meridian Airport is a fictional airport created for this demonstration website. Nothing on this site describes a real place or service.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work. Others track what you do, often for advertising. We only use the first kind, and only one of them.

The cookie we use

This site sets one cookie at most, and only when you open a page with a form. It protects the form from being submitted by another website pretending to be you.

Cookies set by this website
NamePurposeContainsExpires
mer_formProtects forms from forged submissionsA random code. No personal information.When you close your browser

Because this cookie is strictly necessary for the form to work safely, we do not ask for your consent and there is no cookie banner. Pages without a form set no cookies at all.

Cookies we do not use

  • No analytics or statistics cookies
  • No advertising or retargeting cookies
  • No social media cookies
  • No cookies from any other company

The social media links in our footer are plain links. Nothing from those services loads on our pages.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the form cookie, the forms on this site may not work, but every other page will.

Changes

If we ever add a cookie, we will list it here first. This page was last updated on 3 October 2026.
