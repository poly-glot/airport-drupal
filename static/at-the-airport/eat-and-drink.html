At the airport

Eat and drink

Cafés, restaurants and bars before and after security.

Before you start

Most places open by 04:30 for the first flights. After security, venues stay open until the last departure or the time shown. Every venue has gluten-free and vegan choices, and staff can tell you about allergens.

Order ahead

Order from any venue after security in the Meridian Airport app, up to 2 hours before your flight. Pay in the app, and your food or coffee is waiting on the Order ahead shelf by the time you are through security.

Food for the flight

You can take food bought after security on board. Little Orchard and Platform Seven Bakery pack everything to travel. Drinks bought after security can go on board too, but check with your airline whether you can drink them during the flight.

Water

Free water refill points are after security, near every toilet block in both terminals. Bring an empty bottle through security and fill it up on the other side.

Alcohol

Bars serve alcohol from 04:30. Staff will not serve anyone who appears drunk, and airlines can refuse to fly passengers who are. You cannot drink alcohol bought in duty free on board.

Families

The Wayfarer and Skyline Kitchen have high chairs and children's menus. Every venue will warm baby milk or food if you ask.
