At the airport

Lounges and rest

Quiet space, showers and work desks, by the hour.

Hourly lounges

Both terminals have a lounge after security. You pay by the hour, so there is no need to arrive early to get your money's worth.

Book online to guarantee a seat, or walk in if there is space. Pay at the desk when you leave.

What is included

  • Hot breakfast until 11:00, then soup, salads and hot dishes
  • Tea, coffee, soft drinks, beer and wine
  • Fast wifi, charging at every seat and flight screens
  • Newspapers and magazines

The Meridian Lounge, Terminal 1

Open 04:00–21:00. Upstairs from the departure lounge, near gate 6. Lift access. Two shower rooms and eight work desks.

Horizon Lounge, Terminal 2

Open 04:30–21:30. Next to gate 22. A quiet room, a family area with games, three shower rooms and ten work desks.

Fast Track

Skip the main security queue. Fast Track has its own lane in both terminals and costs £7 per person when booked at least 24 hours ahead, or £8 at the entrance on the day. Book it on its own or with a lounge.

How security works

Showers

Shower rooms are inside both lounges and you do not need a lounge booking to use one. A 30-minute slot costs £15 and includes towels, soap, shampoo and a hairdryer. One shower room in each lounge is step-free with a fold-down seat.

Work desks

Desks in both lounges have a power socket, a USB charger and a reading lamp. They are free with lounge entry. Without lounge entry, you can book a desk for £6 an hour.

Quiet Rooms

Free to everyone after security, open 24 hours. Low lights, comfortable seating and no announcements. Find them near gate 4 in Terminal 1 and gate 24 in Terminal 2. Please keep phone calls outside.

Prayer rooms

A multi-faith prayer room is open 24 hours after security in each terminal, with washing facilities next door.

Overnight

The terminals stay open 24 hours. If you have an early flight, you can wait in the check-in hall overnight. Security opens at 03:30. There are no beds in the terminals, but the Quiet Rooms are open all night.

Meridian Airport

Open 24 hours. Passenger enquiries 0121 496 0123, every day 05:00–23:00. Live chat in the app.
