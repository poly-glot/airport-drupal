## Before you book

### Check your terminal

Meridian Air flies from Terminal 1. Nordvik, Azur Express and Kestrel fly from Terminal 2. If you are being dropped off, tell your driver which one.

### Check your bags

Each airline sets its own cabin and hold allowances. Our baggage page has a table for all four.

Link: Baggage allowances by airline -> /help/baggage-and-lost-property.html#allowances

### Check your documents

For flights to the European Union, Switzerland, Norway and Iceland, check that your passport meets the entry rules of the country you are visiting. For Morocco, British passport holders do not need a visa for short stays, but your passport must be valid for your whole visit. Rules change, so check the official government travel advice before you go.

## Booking and changes

We do not sell tickets. Book, change or cancel your flight with your airline. If your flight is cancelled, your airline is responsible for finding you another flight or giving you a refund.
