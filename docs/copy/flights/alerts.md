## How alerts work

1. Enter your flight number and date.
2. Choose text message or WhatsApp, and the alerts you want.
3. We send a short test message to confirm.
4. You get an alert each time something changes, until 2 hours after your flight leaves or lands.

Alerts are free. We only use your number for alerts about the flight you choose, and we delete it 2 hours after the flight.

## What you can get alerts for

### Gate changes

The moment your gate is announced or changes, with the walking time from security.

### Delays and cancellations

A new departure or arrival time as soon as your airline confirms it. If a flight is cancelled, we tell you straight away and point you to your airline.

### Boarding calls

When boarding starts, and a final call 10 minutes before the gate closes.

### Bags on the belt

For arrivals only. A message when the first bags from your flight reach the belt, and which belt it is. Useful if you are meeting someone, too.

## Stop alerts

Reply STOP to any text or WhatsApp message and we will stop straight away. You will not get any more messages about that flight.

## Questions about your flight

Alerts tell you what is happening. To change or cancel a booking, contact your airline. For anything else, call us on 0121 496 0123, every day 05:00–23:00.
