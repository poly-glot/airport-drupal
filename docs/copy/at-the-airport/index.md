## Terminal maps

- Anchor: `#maps`

Meridian Airport has two terminals, linked by a covered walkway. It takes about 5 minutes to walk between them, step-free all the way. Each terminal has its own check-in, security and gates, so check which one your airline uses.

### Terminal 1

Meridian Air flies from Terminal 1.

Map label: Schematic map of Terminal 1: railway station below, check-in zones A to C, security, the departure lounge and gates 1 to 19, and the arrivals hall

Map text: Rail station (level −1) · Check-in A · Check-in B · Check-in C · Security · Departure lounge · Gates 1–10 · Gates 11–19 · Arrivals hall · Walkway to T2 · Lost property

Key points:

- The railway station is one level below check-in. Lifts and escalators come up next to zone A.
- Zone C is for special assistance, oversized bags and anyone who needs more time.
- After security, gates 1 to 10 are a 4-minute walk and gates 11 to 19 are up to 10 minutes.
- The lost property office is in the arrivals hall, next to the taxi rank.

### Terminal 2

Nordvik, Azur Express and Kestrel fly from Terminal 2.

Map label: Schematic map of Terminal 2: check-in zones D to F, security, the departure lounge and gates 20 to 38, the arrivals hall and the coach station

Map text: Check-in D · Check-in E · Check-in F · Security · Departure lounge · Gates 20–29 · Gates 30–38 · Arrivals hall · Coach station · Walkway to T1

Key points:

- Zone D is Nordvik, zone E is Azur Express and zone F is Kestrel. Special assistance is at zone D.
- After security, gates 20 to 29 are a 5-minute walk. Gates 30 to 38 can take up to 15 minutes.
- The coach station is outside the arrivals hall, at the far end from the walkway.

## Facilities in both terminals

### Free wifi

Join "Meridian Free Wifi". No sign-up and no time limit.

### Water and charging

Free water refill points are after security, near every toilet block. Charging points are built into the seating at every gate.

### Toilets and changing

Toilets are before and after security in both terminals. There are Changing Places toilets, with a hoist and adult-sized bench, in the departure lounge of each terminal, and baby changing in every toilet block.

### Prayer and quiet rooms

A multi-faith prayer room is open 24 hours after security in each terminal. Free Quiet Rooms are near gate 4 in Terminal 1 and gate 24 in Terminal 2.

### Money

Cash machines are in both arrivals halls and departure lounges. You can change money at the currency desks after security in both terminals, and order currency online to collect on the day.

### Medical help

Speak to any member of staff, or call 0121 496 0123. First aid teams are on duty 24 hours. Fernhill Pharmacy, before security in Terminal 1, has a pharmacist on site from 05:00 to 21:00.

## Need help on the day?

Information desks are in both check-in halls and both departure lounges, open from the first flight to the last.
