### At the airport

Information desks are in both check-in halls and both departure lounges, open from the first flight to the last.

## Who to contact

Some questions are best answered by someone else. It will be quicker to go straight to them.

- **Bookings, changes, cancellations and refunds:** your airline.
- **A delayed or damaged bag:** your airline's baggage desk in the arrivals hall, before you leave the building.
- **Something left on the aircraft:** your airline.
- **Something left in the terminal, a car park or a shuttle bus:** our lost property office.
- **A parking booking:** us, on 0121 496 0123.
- **Flying with a baby or young children:** read our guide to travelling with children, or ask at any information desk.
- **Flying for the first time:** read our step-by-step guide, or ask at any information desk and we will point you the right way.

## Making a complaint

If something has gone wrong, use the feedback form above and choose the topic that fits. We aim to reply in full within 10 working days. If you are not happy with our reply, ask for your complaint to be reviewed by a senior manager, who will reply within a further 10 working days.
